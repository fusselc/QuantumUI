# COMMUNITY.md

## QuantumUI Community & Open Science
QuantumUI is a collaborative learning and open-science platform for quantum computing.

### Community Principles
- **Curiosity & Inclusivity:** Welcoming to complete beginners with zero math or physics background.
- **Scientific Integrity:** Clear distinction between idealized simulation results and noisy hardware observations.
- **Strict Safety:** Zero tolerance for cryptographic attack or offensive security tooling.
- **Privacy First:** Private experiments remain local. Community dataset participation is strictly opt-in per experiment.

### Public Dataset Sharing
When a user explicitly opts in, the prompt, verified circuit, and execution telemetry are sanitized and added to the open-source dataset under CC BY 4.0 to accelerate global quantum research.
