# CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

## Contributor Code of Conduct
QuantumUI is committed to providing a harassment-free and inclusive experience for everyone.

### Expected Behavior
- Be respectful, collaborative, and constructive.
- Help newcomers learn without gatekeeping.
- Adhere to safety guidelines and respectful discourse.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or trolling.
- Publishing private credentials or personal data.
- Attempting to use the platform for malicious or offensive security attacks.
